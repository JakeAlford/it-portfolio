Monitoring & Discord alerts

Once I had several services running, I wanted to know when one stopped responding instead of finding out by accident.

OperationalIndependent project
Why I did it

Have a straightforward way to check core services and get notified when something needs attention.

What I configured / built
  • Set up Uptime Kuma on a separate Debian Hyper-V VM.
  • Configured checks for home-lab services and network devices.
  • Connected Discord notifications to the monitoring workflow.
What I verified
  • Service monitoring and Discord alerts were tested in the home lab.
What I learned

A failed monitor doesn't tell you everything. It's a reason to investigate DNS, routing, ports or service logs instead of assuming the application itself is broken.

Current status / next steps

Refine the monitoring checks as more services are added.
