Experiments / CASE STUDY

Android device kiosk

I had a spare phone and wanted to see how far I could take it as a dedicated dashboard instead of leaving it unused.

Hands-on experimentIndependent project
Why I did it

Learn about device setup, debugging and the limitations of older Android hardware.

What I configured / built
  • Experimented with a Moto e(7), rooting and Magisk.
  • Used Termux and remote-access tools while planning a kiosk-style view for home-lab services.
  • Worked with device restrictions, app installation and debugging tools.
What I verified
  • Hands-on Android configuration and debugging work was carried out on the spare device.
What I learned

Older devices can still be useful, but compatibility and maintenance matter just as much as getting a dashboard on screen.

Current status / next steps

This is an experiment rather than a production monitoring endpoint.

This is personal project experience, not a claim of paid IT employment. I keep working systems, locally tested features and plans clearly separated.
