Docker & storage experiments

Trying a self-hosted media setup gave me a practical reason to learn Docker, storage mounts and Linux permissions.

Completed / redesignedIndependent project
Why I did it

Understand how multiple services connect to shared storage and why permissions or startup behaviour cause problems.

What I configured / built
  • Trialled Ubuntu Server, Docker and Compose services.
  • Worked with SMB/CIFS network mounts, share permissions and persistence after restarts.
  • Tested parts of a Plex, Sonarr, Radarr and Prowlarr stack and later cleaned up retired test services and shares.
What I verified
  • The storage and service configuration work was tested as an experiment, not retained as a claimed production service.
What I learned

I learned more from sorting out mounts and permissions than from simply installing applications.
