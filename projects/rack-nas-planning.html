Hardware / CASE STUDY

Network rack & NAS planning

As the home lab started taking up more room, I wanted to work out a neater layout that would still be easy to reach and maintain.

Design stageIndependent project
Why I did it

Plan a floor-standing rack that fits existing hardware now and leaves space for a NAS later.

What I configured / built
  • Measured and planned a dark-walnut floor-standing unit with a 9U black open-frame rack section.
  • Accounted for the Aruba PoE switch, patch panel, upright Dell server and audio equipment.
  • Compared diskless four-bay NAS options as a possible future addition.
What I verified
  • A measured design was prepared; the rack and NAS are not being represented as completed installations.
What I learned

Physical space, airflow, cable access and future maintenance all affect a good infrastructure layout.

Current status / next steps

Build and verify the physical rack, then select NAS hardware when appropriate.
