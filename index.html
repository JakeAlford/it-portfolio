03 / APPROACH

How I troubleshoot.

I learn a lot from things not working first time. This is the approach I try to follow when something goes wrong.

01Reproduce it

Work out what is happening and when the problem appears.

02Check the basics

Connectivity, logs, settings and whether the service is actually running.

03Change one thing

Test a possible fix without losing track of what changed.

04Check again

Verify the result, keep a note and roll back experiments if needed.
